<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Holong Na Maila</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sian pamereng mu tu au<br>Uboto do hasian<br>Holong ni roham tu au<br>Jala unang sai tabunihon<br>Holong na adong di rohami</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Simalolongmu dang boi<br>Margabus da hasian<br>Tarida sian pamerengmu<br>Na holong roham tu au ito da nalagu</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bahen suratmu tu au, rade do au mambalos i<br>Jala haholonganhu do ho sian bagas ni rohakku<br>Simalolongmu dohot bohimi manghusiphon na dirohami<br>Alai bibirmu dang olo mandok na dirohami</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unang ma mailaila ho<br>Jala unang sai tutupi holongmi<br>Hatahon na dibagas rohami</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
