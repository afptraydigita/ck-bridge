<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Hagabeon</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hagabeon, hasangapon, hamoraon<br>Nasian tuhan i do i<br>Jala unang lupa ho martangian<br>Mandok mauliate<br>Disude denggan ni basa na<br>Jala unang mian diroham<br>Akka jat ni roha</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sai paserep ma roham tabolokkon<br>Tois ni roha i<br>Jala ta bolokkon ma sian rohatta<br>Elat teal hosomi<br>Asa taruli ho dingolumi<br>Di tano parsatokkinan on<br>Dihasinangan on</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hagabeon, hasangapon, hamoraon<br>Tinggal doi sude<br>Molo dung pajuppang<br>Molo dung pajuppang<br>Molo dung pajuppang<br>Hamateani</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ulahon na denggan<br>Bissan mangolu ho<br>Songon panakko borngin<br>Haroro nai<br>Jala dang adong mangambati<br>Ai guru di ibana<br>Langit tano on<br>Lan pe di tahi<br>Akka jolma i<br>Rohana do na saut</p>
