<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Marinahon Parjuji</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Baligama nibaligahon amang<br>Baritama di baritahon tu au<br>Naung balik hape portibi amang<br>Ala ni pangalaho ni parumaenhi<br>Gabe sursar parsaripeon mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Boasa ma amang dibuni bunihon ho<br>Pangalaho ni parumaen hi<br>Nunga bagianmu amang ikkon songonon<br>marinahon parjuji sude pahoppukki<br>boha bahenon nunga turpukmu i amang</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Amang mulak maho,amang tu huta on<br>Boan ma mulak amang, sude pahoppuki<br>Unang marsikkori, diparjalangan i<br>Tumagon namai, molo somubabe parumaen hi</p>
