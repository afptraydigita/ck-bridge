<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Lomos Ni Simatua - Perdana Trio</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unang sai lomos be roham<br>Inang simatuakku<br>Tung godang manggoda au dipardalankki<br>Tu boru mi do rohakki</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hu ingot do ale inang<br>Di poda ni inang i sipangintubu i<br>Ia boru mi inang parumaen na tinodo</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dongan hu gabe sdaur matua<br>Nang pe ganjang dalan sidalanakki<br>Toktong do bohii ni boru mi<br>Dohot pahompu mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Na sai mian di matakki<br>Pinasada ni Tuhan i<br>Tung so boi sirangon i<br>Pos ma roham inang boru mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dongan gabe saur matua</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
