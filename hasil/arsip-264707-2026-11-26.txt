<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Di Tugu Monas</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dang tarjalo ahu ito namasaon<br>Parsirangan na binahenmon tu ahu<br>Tarsongon namarnipi ahu nang pe somodom<br>Di namanjalo gokhon jou-jou mi<br>Naso patut na so uhum ma dibahen ho<br>Pangarohai mi di ahu dahasian</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dang tarbereng ahu ito parekkel mi<br>Di nahundul ho dohot sidoli i<br>Marlojong ahu ito lao manjonohi ho<br>Naeng manggantihon baoa na dilambung mi<br>Hape ro natoropi mangelek-elek ahu<br>Manogu ahu mambaon ahu sian jolo mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Selendang sutra na binoan mi ito<br>Dohot kabaya na hutuhor i di ho<br>Dipakke ho ma ito dipesta mi<br>Pasudahon rohangki</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Baju pangoli napinillit mi di ahu<br>Dohot sipatu nalao sipakkeon hi<br>Gantunghonon hu namai ditugu monas<br>Tugu na timbo i</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Asa sude pardalan i tahe umboto<br>Naung muli ho ito<br>Asa sude pamolus i tahe marnida<br>Naso saut ahu tu ho</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Di Tugu Monas</strong></p>
