<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Lupa Do Ho</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Marudur do sude<br>Akka dongan sahuta da<br>Ditopi pari di hutai<br>Na di toru ni bului</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nang ilu ilu pe huhut<br>Sai maraburani<br>Lao paborhathon ho<br>Naujui tuparjalangan</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Makkirim do sude<br>Haha anggi iboto mi<br>Anggiat sahat ho hasian<br>Tu tinodo ni rohami</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Anak siparbagaon tahe<br>Sian na dihutai<br>Ai tung makkirim do<br>Sude amang digogo mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ai dung tarida ma<br>Dagoarmi amang<br>Sahat tu ujung ni portibion<br>Ai gabe lupa do</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ho di lage lage<br>Podoman mi naung maribaki<br>Nang didalan na margamboi amang<br>Manang ditopi ni sappilpili</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ima tano ha tubuan mi amang<br>Lupa doho<br>Manimbung au amang<br>Da tualamani</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sian balatukni jabuttai<br>Hurippu do amang<br>Ho ro mulak I<br>Manduloi hutam nabunii</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Holan uap na<br>So marippolai<br>Namarbagahon hapistaran mi<br>Ima upani namaranakkoni o amonge<br>Ima upani namanubuhoni dangol nai</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Lupa Do Ho</strong></p>
