<p>Nungga huboan be inang parumaen mi<br>Siboru tinodo ni rohakkki<br>Boru ni raja boru na mora<br>Mora marroha</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Parumaen naung leleng pinaima mi<br>Nagabe sirokkap ni tondikku<br>Dongan marluga disada solu<br>Dingolu-ngolu</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Paherbang ma tanganmu<br>Ale amang ale inang<br>Lehon pasu-pasu tu hamion anakkonmon<br>Anggiat ma dituppak tuhan i<br>Marpinoppar jala gabe hami sogot</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Parumaen na gabe songon panggatti mi<br>Lao pature lao paondihon au<br>Dongan saroha sisada hata disada ngolu</p>
