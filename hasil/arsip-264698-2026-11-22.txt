<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Baju Nabirong</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sai tu matangis ho diabingan hi<br>Lao patolhashon na solot di roha mi<br>Ikkon marsirang do hita na dua<br>Na marsihaholongan</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sai tarhatotong au ito da hasian<br>Umbege i sude na hata mi<br>Ho mandok muli, o mandok muli<br>Tu naso lomo mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Di pulo batam na parpudi<br>Hu tuhor baju parsirangan i<br>Laos di patollhas ho tu au<br>Nasolot di roha mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hotel mandarin na gabe saksi<br>Di pakke homa baju na birong i<br>Mekkel suping ho mamereng au<br>Laos hu galmit ma ito da hurum mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ndada sitodo turpuk<br>Si ahut lomo ni roha<br>Ima si ingoton mu<br>Hata parpudi sian au</p>
