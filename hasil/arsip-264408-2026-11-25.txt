<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Tataring Parapian</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sogot manogoti ale amang<br>Hehe au sian podomanhi<br>Tatataring parapiani<br>Sai hudadang tangn hi<br>Huhut huingot ho amang</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Huloppit tanganhi ale amang<br>Martangiang au tu tuhan i<br>Anggiat nian turpuk mu i<br>Passamotan nadenggan<br>Diparjalangan mi amang</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jotjot tarilu au molo hurimangi i<br>Nasibhu namargeleng i<br>Sipata olo au<br>Mangapian rohakki<br>Marnida dongan sahuta i</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Soartami amang<br>Nanaeng haliangan hi<br>Anak hasian nalagu<br>Holan patuduhon pe<br>Paboa namargelleng au<br>Nunga balga be rohakki amang</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Hape nian amang<br>Ngasundut be amang<br>Mata niarikki</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Satokkin nai nama<br>Salpu nama amang<br>Salpu nama portibi<br>Sian inang mu on</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
