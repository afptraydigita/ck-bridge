<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Saputangan Na Marsap Ilu</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nunga tung leleng hita naung mardongan<br>Nunga tung leleng hita naung marpadan<br>Tung naso jadi marsirang, rap dinasonang<br>Susa rap manaon ido parjanjian</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Tung massai tompu do parguling ni roha mi<br>Gabe lupa ho dipadan naung tabahen i<br>Tung so huboto mandok ito,dung ditongos ho<br>Surat mi tu au paboa naung mangoli ho</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dang natarhatahon au ito arsak ni rohakki<br>Dungkon hujaha isi ni surat dohot undangan mi<br>Sipata naeng hutimbung ito lombang na bagasi<br>Unang huingot ingoti nahassit nabinahen mi<br>Dang targabei au be ho tu si boru pinillit mi</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ndang boi ahu ro mandulo ho tu pesta mi<br>Ndang tarbahen ahu laho mangadopi gokhon mi<br>Jalo ma i tinongoshi ito saputangan na marsap ilukki<br>Ima tuppak hi tu ho</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
