<p><strong>Lirik Lagu Batak: Burju Ma Ho Marsimatua</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Tinggalhononmu ma au boru<br>Hami natua-tuamon<br>Ai nunga ro be rokappmi<br>Natinodo ni rohami<br>Borhat ma ho, tiur ma lakkami<br>Manang didia pe, marhuta ho muse</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Di tangiang ma boru<br>Hita mardomu hasian<br>Ihuthononmu nama i<br>Helakku siadohon mi<br>Horas ma ho, uli ma nipimi<br>Di hokkop tondikki, ditumpaki tuhan i</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Tubuan laklak dainang, tubu sikkoru<br>Tubuan anak ho inang, tubuan boru<br>Asa marluga ma hamu, di sada solu<br>Mangalului na rikkot, dalan ni ngolu</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sai hot ma ho di hatuaonmi<br>Tadikkon haposoon tua pangalahon</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Malo malo ma ho boru marsiadopan<br>Marsianjuan ma hamu, marsada roha<br>Burju burju ho borukku marsimatua<br>Asa tanda ma ho inang boru ni raja</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
